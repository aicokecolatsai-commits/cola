# 人因工程圖 — 正確與錯誤站姿

說明：此檔案包含可直接用於 AI 圖像生成器的提示詞（英文）以及簡短的版面說明，目標產出一張對比圖，左側為「正確站姿」，右側展示多種常見的「錯誤站姿」並附簡短標註與改善建議。

---

提示詞（英文，直接貼入圖像生成器）：
"Infographic poster showing ergonomic standing posture: left panel titled 'Correct Posture' with a neutral, upright human figure (side and front view), head aligned over shoulders, natural S-curve spine, relaxed knees, weight evenly distributed on both feet, feet hip-width apart. Right panel titled 'Incorrect Postures' showing 4 small vignettes: 1) Slouched/rounded shoulders, 2) Forward head posture, 3) Locked knees and hyperextension, 4) Weight shifted to one leg/pelvic tilt. Use clean flat vector style, high-contrast annotations, arrows and short labels pointing to problem areas, short corrective tips under each vignette. Color palette: muted teal for correct (greenish), warm red/orange for incorrect, neutral background. Typography: clear sans-serif, legible labels. Include icons for spine, feet, and clock for tips. Layout: A4 portrait 2480x3508 px, margin for printing. Minimal realistic human anatomy, gender-neutral figures, accessible design, high readability."

可選的 negative prompt（減少不必要細節）：
"photorealistic, heavy shadows, busy background, text-heavy, low-resolution, noisy"

---

版面與使用建議：
- 建議尺寸：A4 2480×3508 px（印刷）或 1920×1080 px（簡報）。
- 色彩與標註：正確姿勢使用冷色（綠/藍），錯誤姿勢使用暖色（紅/橘），箭頭與標籤使用深色加白色描邊以提高可讀性。
- 標註內容範例（每個錯誤旁）：
  - Slouched: "Rounded shoulders → Tight chest muscles, weak upper back"
  - Forward head: "Forward head → Neck strain, increased disc load"
  - Locked knees: "Locked knees → Reduced shock absorption, hyperextension"
  - Weight shift: "Shifted weight → Pelvic tilt, uneven load"
- 底部可加一行簡短改善建議："Keep chin tucked, shoulders relaxed, micro-breaks every 30–60 min, alternate weight evenly."
